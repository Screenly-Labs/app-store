Celebrate birthdays on your screens

The Birthday app puts a friendly birthday celebration on any display, so a team or a venue can mark the day on screen. It is a warm, personal touch for shared spaces.

Where it fits

  • Office reception and break-room screens
  • Schools, clubs, and community centres
  • Hospitality and care settings

How to add the birthday app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player. See also team milestones for work anniversaries.
