Every time zone on one screen

The World Clock app puts a clean world clock display on any screen, showing several cities and time zones side by side and keeping them accurate around the clock. It's built for teams and places that work across borders.

Who uses a multi–time-zone clock?

  • Global offices coordinating calls across regions
  • Trading floors, newsrooms, and operations centres
  • Hotel lobbies and airports serving international visitors
  • Remote-first teams that want everyone's local time in view

How to add the world clock app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free, open-source, Raspberry Pi), Screenly, or any player that can show a web page. Pick your cities and the screen does the rest.

Can it show more than one time zone at once?

Yes — that's the point. The world clock display lays out multiple zones together so you can read them all at a glance.

Is it free and does it run on Raspberry Pi?

Both. On Anthias the world clock runs free on a Raspberry Pi you already have.
