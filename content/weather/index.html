Live weather on any screen

The Weather app turns any display into clean, always-current weather signage: current conditions plus the days ahead, refreshed automatically for the location you choose. There's nothing to design and nothing to maintain — set it once and the screen keeps itself up to date.

Where a weather app earns its place

  • Reception and lobby screens, so visitors see the local forecast at a glance
  • Office and break-room displays alongside other signage apps
  • Schools, gyms, and co-working spaces planning the day around the weather
  • Retail and hospitality screens that pair the forecast with promotions

How to add the weather app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias — the free, open-source digital signage player for Raspberry Pi — into Screenly, or into any smart TV or media player that can show a web page. No account or design work required.

Is the weather app free?

Yes. Run it on Anthias and your weather signage is completely free and self-hosted on a Raspberry Pi you already own.

Does the weather app work on Raspberry Pi?

It does. Anthias runs on Raspberry Pi and displays the weather app full-screen, so a single Pi becomes a dedicated weather board.
