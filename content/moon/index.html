Tonight's moon, on any screen

The Moon Phase app shows a live moon phase display — the current phase and illumination, updated each day. It's a simple, striking way to fill a screen with something people look twice at.

Where it fits

  • Science centres, planetariums, and classrooms
  • Nature reserves, marinas, and coastal venues tracking tides and light
  • Ambient office and lobby screens between other signage apps

How to add the moon phase app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.

Is it free?

Yes — it's free to run on Anthias, the open-source signage player.
