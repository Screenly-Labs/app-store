Live air quality on screen

The Air Quality app shows a live air quality display for your location — the current AQI and conditions, updated automatically and colour-coded so the reading is clear from across the room.

Where it's useful

  • Office and reception screens during wildfire-smoke or high-pollution days
  • Schools and gyms deciding on outdoor activity
  • Building lobbies and co-working spaces as a wellbeing signal
  • Alongside the weather app for a full local conditions board

How to add the air quality app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any player that can display a web page.

Is the air quality app free?

Yes — it's free to run on Anthias, the open-source signage player.
