Help find missing older adults in Florida from any screen

The Florida Silver Alert app turns a screen into a Silver Alert board. Each time it loads, it shows one person from the Florida Department of Law Enforcement's current Silver Alerts: their photo, their age, when and where they went missing, and a QR code that opens the official FDLE flyer on a phone. The newest alerts come first.

Silver Alerts help find older adults with dementia who have gone missing, often by car. "Call 911 or 1-888-FL-MISSING" is on screen at all times.

Where it fits

  • Gas stations, convenience stores, and rest areas
  • Pharmacies, grocery stores, and shopping centers
  • Senior centers, libraries, and community centers
  • Hospital and clinic waiting rooms

Always current, never stale

The alert list is refreshed from FDLE every hour and checked field by field before it is published. If the list hasn't been refreshed for a few hours, or the screen is offline, the app shows the phone numbers instead of an old alert, so a person who has been found never stays on screen.

Alert data and photographs come from FDLE and are shown unaltered. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by FDLE.

How to add the Florida Silver Alert app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.
