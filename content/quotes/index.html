A rotating wall of quotes

The Quotes app cycles a rotating set of quotes on any screen — motivational lines, company values, or workplace safety quotes — with clean typography and automatic rotation, so a screen always has something worth reading.

Where a quotes board fits

  • Offices and break rooms — culture, values, and motivation
  • Factory and warehouse floors — rotating workplace safety reminders
  • Schools and gyms — daily inspiration
  • Reception screens — a warmer, more human welcome

How to add the quotes app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free, open-source, Raspberry Pi), Screenly, or any signage player that shows web content.

Can I use it for workplace safety quotes?

Yes — it's a common use. Rotate safety reminders on a shop-floor screen the same way you would motivational quotes.

Is it free?

Yes. On Anthias the quotes display runs free on your own Raspberry Pi.
