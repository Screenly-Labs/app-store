A big, clear clock on any screen

The Digital Clock app fills any display with a large, always-accurate clock, so the time reads across a room at a glance. It sets itself from the network and needs no configuration.

Where a clock screen fits

  • Reception areas, lobbies, and waiting rooms
  • Studios, control rooms, and production floors that run to the clock
  • Classrooms, gyms, and shared offices

How to add the clock app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any signage player that shows a web page. Working across regions? See the world clock for multiple time zones.
