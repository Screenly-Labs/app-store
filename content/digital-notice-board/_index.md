A cork board says whatever somebody last pinned to it. A **digital notice board** says whatever you published this morning, and it says it to everybody walking past.

You do not need special hardware, and you do not need to install anything on the display. Everything here is a web page, so any screen that can open a URL will do: a smart TV, a streaming stick, a Raspberry Pi, or a browser in kiosk mode.
