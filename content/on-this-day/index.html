Today in history, on your screen

The On This Day app shows notable events, births, and milestones that happened on today's date, refreshed each day. It is a simple way to give a screen a talking point that changes on its own.

Where it fits

  • Schools, museums, and libraries
  • Office and break-room screens
  • Reception and waiting-area displays

How to add the on this day app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.
