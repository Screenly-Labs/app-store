A rotating geography quiz on screen

The Capital Quiz app shows a country and its capital in a clean, rotating quiz format. It is a light, engaging way to fill a screen and give people something to test themselves on.

Where it fits

  • Classrooms and school corridors
  • Libraries, museums, and visitor centres
  • Break rooms and waiting areas

How to add the capital quiz app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.
