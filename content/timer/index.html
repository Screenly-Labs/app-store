A big, readable timer on any screen

The Timer app shows a large countdown timer or count-up timer on any display — counting down to a moment, or counting up from one. It's readable across a room and needs no setup beyond the target time.

Ways teams use it

  • Event and webinar countdowns — "starting in…" on the room screen
  • Product launches, sales, and campaign deadlines
  • "Days since last incident" safety boards on factory and warehouse floors
  • Sprints, workshops, and classroom activities kept on schedule

How to add the timer app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free, open-source, Raspberry Pi), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player, then set your target time.

Does it count up as well as down?

Yes — use it as a countdown to an event or a count-up for things like "days since last incident".

Is the timer app free?

Yes. With Anthias on a Raspberry Pi it runs free with no subscription.
