A new word on screen every day

The Word of the Day app shows a fresh word with its definition and pronunciation, refreshed daily. It is an easy way to keep a screen interesting and give people something to learn in passing.

Where it fits

  • Schools, libraries, and language classrooms
  • Office break rooms and reception screens
  • Any ambient display that benefits from rotating content

How to add the word of the day app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.
