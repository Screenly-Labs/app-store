Your menu, priced and grouped, on any screen

The Menu Board app turns a screen behind the counter into a menu your customers can read from across the room. Group items into sections, give each one a price and an optional line of description, and the board lays itself out and scales to fit the display.

There is no dashboard and no account. The whole menu travels in the app link, so changing a price means editing the link and the board updates the next time it loads.

Where it fits

  • Cafes and coffee shops, above the counter or in the window
  • Bars, taprooms, and food trucks
  • Delis, bakeries, and small kitchens
  • Canteens and staff cafeterias

Built to be read at three metres

A menu board fails quietly when an item scrolls out of sight, so this one never scrolls. It moves to a second column as the menu grows and shrinks the type until everything fits, on a 4K screen in the window or a small display by the till, portrait or landscape.

How to add the menu board app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.
