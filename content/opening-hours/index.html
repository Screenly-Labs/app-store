Your opening hours, clearly on screen

The Opening Hours app shows your business hours in a clean, easy-to-read layout, so customers know at a glance when you are open. Set it once and leave it running in a window or entrance.

Where it fits

  • Shop windows, entrances, and reception desks
  • Cafes, restaurants, and salons
  • Clinics, offices, and service counters

How to add the opening hours app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player.
