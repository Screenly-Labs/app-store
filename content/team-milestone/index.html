Recognise milestones on your screens

The Team Milestone app celebrates work anniversaries and team achievements on any display, so recognition happens in a shared space where everyone sees it.

Where it fits

  • Office reception, break rooms, and team areas
  • Onboarding and all-hands displays
  • Clubs, schools, and community groups

How to add the team milestone app

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any web-capable signage player. See also the birthday app.
