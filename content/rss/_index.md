## Live news and RSS feeds on any screen

Turn any display into a live news board. These are free, ready-made **RSS feeds for digital signage**: each one auto-rotates through the latest stories from a single source, one at a time with its headline, image, and time, refreshed automatically. Pick a feed, paste its link into your player, and the screen keeps itself current with no work from you.

## News, sports, tech, and more

Choose from major news outlets like CNN, BBC, NBC, Fox, NPR, and The Guardian; sports from ESPN, Sky Sports, and Yahoo Sports; technology from The Verge, Ars Technica, and Hacker News; plus visual feeds like NASA's Image of the Day. Every feed below is a one-click add.

## How to add a feed

Open any feed below, copy its app link, and paste it into [Anthias](https://anthias.screenly.io/?utm_source=signage-apps.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=rss-hub) (free and open-source), Screenly, or any signage player that displays a web page. No account and no design work required.
