NASA on your screens

This app puts the NASA - Image of the Day feed on any display as a full-screen image with its caption, refreshed automatically on a loop. It is a ready-made visual feed for digital signage, so there is nothing to build or maintain.

How to add the NASA feed

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any signage player that shows a web page. Browse the full set of news and RSS feeds for digital signage for more outlets.
