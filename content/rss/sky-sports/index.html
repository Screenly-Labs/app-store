Sky Sports on your screens

This app puts the Sky Sports feed on any display: one story at a time with its headline, image, source, and time, refreshed automatically. It is a ready-made sports RSS feed for digital signage, so there is nothing to build or maintain.

How to add the Sky Sports feed

Copy the app link above and paste it into Anthias (free and open-source), Screenly, or any signage player that shows a web page. Browse the full set of news and RSS feeds for digital signage for more outlets.
